FUMES 1.0.4 -- short changelog for comments / the upload form
=============================================================

1.0.4 is up. Three bug fixes from the comments.

  - Bikes ignored every consumption setting you changed. A bike's thirst
    is worked out from its tank and range, and that was returning over
    the top of the class figure -- so the CLASSES page showed your number
    while the burn used another. Change it now and yours wins. Pinning a
    bike by name works too. @AliG_15

  - The gauge was placed for whatever resolution the game first reported
    and never looked again, so fullscreen and windowed could not both be
    right. It also dragged Bare Minimum's row off the side of the screen.
    Measured once a second now. @paparumz

  - No route to a station if you already had a waypoint set. The route is
    drawn on the station's own blip and never touched your waypoint, so
    standing aside was pointless. Both lines draw now. @AliG_15

247 settings. Drop it over the old one, nothing needs a new save.
