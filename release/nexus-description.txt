<b>Persistent vehicle fuel for GTA V, with a refuel you walk through.</b>

Every vehicle carries a real fuel tank. It empties as you drive, and it is still empty when you come back to that car tomorrow.

<b>Free.</b> Open source under MIT. Nightly builds at <a href="https://spitmux.me">spitmux.me</a> if you want it before it lands here.

===================================

<b>WHAT IT IS</b>

<b>Refuelling is not a menu.</b> Walk to the pump, take the nozzle. It goes in your hand and the hose comes with you. Walk round to that model's filler, fill up, walk the nozzle back and hang it up. Walk too far and the hose pulls it out of your hands.

Runs on <b>Legacy and Enhanced from one build</b>.

===================================

<b>WHAT ELSE IS IN THERE</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Siphon fuel out of any car</b> with a jerry can, or fill the can at a pump;</li>
<li><b>A full can does not stop it.</b> The overflow pools at your feet, and it ignites;</li>
<li><b>Fuel grades: Regular, Plus, Premium.</b> The grade stays in the tank. A card at the pump shows what each costs there;</li>
<li><b>Diesel</b> for trucks, buses, tractors and the fire engine. Costs more, goes further;</li>
<li><b>Bikes get a bike's tank</b>, about seventeen dollars to fill, same range as a car;</li>
<li><b>Electric vehicles are left alone entirely.</b> No tank, no gauge, no fuel burned. Detected by the model's own flag, so DLC and add-on electrics too;</li>
<li><b>Traffic pulls into forecourts</b> when it is low and already nearby;</li>
<li><b>The gauge sloshes with speed</b>, chimes on reserve, and hides with the game's HUD, in cutscenes and through fades;</li>
<li><b>Nine languages</b> from the settings menu: English (UK, US), Português (BR), Español, Français, Deutsch, Русский, Polski, Hindi, 中文. English by default; anything a translation does not cover shows in English. Picking one sets the units its speakers expect. Each is one json file, so corrections and new languages need no rebuild;</li>
<li><b>Twenty-five stations on the map</b>, one legend entry between them. Refuelling itself does not use the list: the mod finds the pump you are stood at by its prop, so a station missing from the map still works.</li>
</ul>

===================================

<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Script Hook V</b> for your edition;</li>
<li><b>ScriptHookVDotNet 3</b>, any build from <b>3.6.0</b> up. Nightlies and the Enhanced fork included.</li>
</ul>

On <b>GTA V Enhanced</b> it must be the Enhanced fork: <a href="https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/script-hook-v-net-enhanced">script-hook-v-net-enhanced</a>

The Enhanced fork reports 3.9.0.0 but is released as v1.1.x. To see what you have: right-click <i>ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll</i>, Properties, Details.

Nothing else is needed. <b>Remove any other fuel mod first.</b> Two fuel mods will both cut your engine and both charge you, and neither will know about the other.

<b>Compatibility.</b> No game files are touched, so graphics, map and vehicle packs are fine. Built alongside <b>Posted Up</b> and <b>Bare Minimum</b> and tested with both running. If you run Vehicle Tweaks as well, its ignition and this mod's empty-tank stall are both pulling on the same engine; turn one of them off on an empty tank.

<b>Bare Minimum is not required.</b> If it is there the gauge joins its row of bars as a sixth bar, matched to it and moving with it. If it is not, the gauge stands on its own exactly as it always did. Nothing is referenced by name at build time, so an absent, older or newer Bare Minimum cannot stop this mod loading.

===================================

<b>INSTALLING</b>

Copy the <i>scripts</i> folder from the zip into your GTA V folder. You end up with <i>Fumes.dll</i>, <i>Fumes.ini</i> and a <i>Fumes</i> folder inside <i>scripts</i>. The <i>Fumes\icons</i> folder is the HUD's pictures, keep it.

To uninstall, delete those. Nothing else is written and nothing in the game's files is touched.

<b>No log at all?</b> The mod writes <i>scripts\Fumes\Fumes.log</i> on startup. If it is not there the DLL never ran, which is nearly always ScriptHookVDotNet 3 missing or older than 3.6.0.

===================================

<b>CONTROLS</b>

<ul>
<li><b>SHIFT + F</b>: the settings menu, 246 of them. <b>LB + D-pad Down</b> on a controller;</li>
<li><b>E</b>: take the nozzle, fill, hang up, pour the can. <b>Q</b>: siphon, or fill the can at a pump.</li>
</ul>
