<b>FUMES 0.1.3</b>
Persistent vehicle fuel for GTA V, with a refuel you walk through.
by spitmux


<b>WHAT IT IS</b>

Every vanilla and online vehicle carries a real fuel tank. It empties as you drive and it is still empty when you come back to that car tomorrow.

Refuelling is not a menu. Get out, walk to the pump, take the nozzle off it — it goes in your hand and the hose comes with you. Walk round to wherever that model's filler actually is, fill up, then walk the nozzle back and hang it on the pump. Walk too far and the hose pulls it out of your hands.

Runs on <b>both editions</b>, Legacy and Enhanced, from one build.

<b>SHIFT+F</b> opens the settings menu, or <b>LB + D-pad Down</b> on a controller.


<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

<ul>
<li>Script Hook V, for your edition</li>
<li>ScriptHookVDotNet 3 — <b>any build from 3.6.0</b>, including the nightlies and the Enhanced fork</li>
</ul>

On <b>GTA V Enhanced</b> it has to be the Enhanced fork, because nothing else runs on that edition at all: <a href="https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/script-hook-v-net-enhanced">ScriptHookVDotNet Enhanced</a>

Nothing else. No NativeUI, no LemonUI, no iFruitAddon.


<b>INSTALLING</b>

Copy the <b>scripts</b> folder from this zip into your GTA V folder, so you end up with:

<ul>
<li>Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.dll</li>
<li>Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.ini</li>
<li>Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\stations.json</li>
<li>Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\icons\*.png</li>
</ul>

The icons folder is not optional decoration — without it the HUD still works but has no pictures in it.

To uninstall, delete those. Nothing is written anywhere else, and nothing in the game's own files is touched.


<b>CHANGELOG — 0.1.3</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Runs on any ScriptHookVDotNet 3 from 3.6.0 now</b>, not just the Enhanced fork. It goes through the native underneath now.</li>

<li><b>Diesel.</b> Trucks, buses, tractors and the fire engine take it. Costs more per litre, goes further.</li>
<li><b>Fuel grades</b> — Regular, Plus, Premium. The grade lives in the tank, so a car you filled with premium is still running on premium.</li>
<li><b>Controller support</b> for the settings menu and the grade card.</li>
<li><b>Traffic pulls into forecourts</b> when it is low and already nearby.</li>

<li>The gauge sloshes with speed, throws to one side when you stop hard, and hides when the game's HUD does.</li>
