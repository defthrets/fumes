FUMES 0.1.3
Persistent vehicle fuel for GTA V, with a refuel you walk through.
by spitmux


WHAT IT IS
==========

Every vanilla and online vehicle carries a real fuel tank. It empties as you
drive and it is still empty when you come back to that car tomorrow.

Refuelling is not a menu. Get out, walk to the pump, take the nozzle off it --
it goes in your hand and the hose comes with you. Walk round to wherever that
model's filler actually is, fill up, then walk the nozzle back and hang it on
the pump. Walk too far and the hose pulls it out of your hands.

Runs on BOTH editions, Legacy and Enhanced, from one build.

SHIFT+F opens the settings menu, or LB + D-pad Down on a controller.


REQUIREMENTS
============

  Script Hook V           for your edition

  ScriptHookVDotNet 3     any build from 3.6.0, including the nightlies
                          and including the Enhanced fork

On GTA V ENHANCED it has to be the Enhanced fork, because nothing else runs on
that edition at all:

  https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/script-hook-v-net-enhanced

Nothing else. No NativeUI, no LemonUI, no iFruitAddon.


INSTALLING
==========

Copy the "scripts" folder from this zip into your GTA V folder, so you end up
with:

  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.dll
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.ini
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\stations.json
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\icons\*.png

The icons folder is not optional decoration -- without it the HUD still works
but has no pictures in it.

To uninstall, delete those. Nothing is written anywhere else, and nothing in
the game's own files is touched.


CHANGELOG - 0.1.3
=================

  - Runs on any ScriptHookVDotNet 3 from 3.6.0 now, not just the Enhanced fork.
    It called one method that only newer builds have, in five different places,
    so on older builds it loaded fine and then failed later in five unrelated
    spots without ever saying why. It goes through the native underneath now.

  - Siphon fuel out of any car. Walk up to a filler with a jerry can and draw
    fuel the other way. He crouches, stands the can down in front of him, and
    runs a hose from his hand to its spout.

  - A full can does not stop it. The hose has no idea, so a pool of petrol grows
    at your feet. It is the game's own petrol, which means it ignites.

  - A grade card at the pump, showing what each grade costs at THAT station and
    what it returns.

  - Diesel. Trucks, buses, tractors and the fire engine take it. Costs more per
    litre, goes further.

  - Fuel grades: Regular, Plus, Premium. The grade lives in the tank, so a car
    you filled with premium is still running on premium.

  - Controller support for the settings menu and the grade card.

  - Traffic pulls into forecourts when it is low and already nearby.

  - You can throw a punch mid-siphon.

  - A chime when you dip into reserve. Once per dip.

  - The gauge sloshes with speed, throws to one side when you stop hard, and
    hides when the game's HUD does.

  - Fixed: help text was losing a space at every line break, which welded button
    icons onto the word behind them.

  - Fixed: story-mission cars are full again, without DLC cars going back to
    being full.

  - 207 settings in the ini, up from 118, every one commented with why it is
    there.
