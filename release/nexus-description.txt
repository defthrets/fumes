FUMES 0.1.4
Persistent vehicle fuel for GTA V, with a refuel you walk through.
by spitmux


WHAT IT IS
==========

Every vanilla and online vehicle carries a real fuel tank. It empties as you
drive and it is still empty when you come back to that car tomorrow.

Refuelling is not a menu. Get out, walk to the pump, take the nozzle off it --
it goes in your hand and the hose comes with you. Walk round to wherever that
model's filler actually is, fill up, then walk the nozzle back and hang it on
the pump. Walk too far and the hose pulls it out of your hands.

Runs on BOTH editions, Legacy and Enhanced, from one build.

SHIFT+F opens the settings menu, or LB + D-pad Down on a controller.


REQUIREMENTS
============

  Script Hook V           for your edition

  ScriptHookVDotNet 3     any build from 3.6.0, including the nightlies
                          and including the Enhanced fork

On GTA V ENHANCED it has to be the Enhanced fork, because nothing else runs on
that edition at all:

  https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/script-hook-v-net-enhanced

Nothing else. No NativeUI, no LemonUI, no iFruitAddon.


INSTALLING
==========

Copy the "scripts" folder from this zip into your GTA V folder, so you end up
with:

  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.dll
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes.ini
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\stations.json
  Grand Theft Auto V\scripts\Fumes\icons\*.png

The icons folder is not optional decoration -- without it the HUD still works
but has no pictures in it.

To uninstall, delete those. Nothing is written anywhere else, and nothing in
the game's own files is touched.


CHANGELOG - 0.1.4
=================

  - Fixed: a vehicle could come back from a save load with a FULL TANK when it
    had half of one. Two things have to go wrong together and a load is the
    one moment they both do. The tank is looked up by number plate, and a car
    that has just streamed in may not have its plate yet -- so the saved level
    is not found. At the same moment the game's mission flag is set while it
    restores itself, and the rule that hands a full tank to a car a mission
    gives you fires on that.
    The mission rule now needs a plate. A vehicle that cannot be identified
    cannot be known to be a mission vehicle, and "full" is the most damaging
    thing to guess about a car you do not recognise. Reported by sjmss8094.

  - BIKES GET A BIKE'S TANK. Sixteen litres rather than the sixty-five that
    handling.meta reports for them, so filling one costs about seventeen
    dollars instead of eighty.
    And they still go as far as a car. The economy figure is worked out from
    the tank size and the range together rather than listed, so a quarter of
    the fuel covers the same ground -- and if you change one the other
    follows.

  - ELECTRIC VEHICLES ARE OUT of the mod entirely. No tank, no gauge, no
    prompt at a pump, no fuel burned. A Voltic taking sixty litres of unleaded
    was the one thing in here that was simply wrong. AffectElectric turns them
    back on if you want the old behaviour. Asked for by MaitreSystem.

  - ALL THE STATIONS ARE ONE ENTRY on the map legend now, instead of one row
    each. The legend groups by name, so twenty-six forecourts were twenty-six
    of the rows you page through. The cost is that the row no longer names the
    brand or the district; BlipName blank gives that back. Asked for by
    enbee997.

  - Fixed: he filled up with the wrong hand. The nozzle was already in his
    right hand -- that setting only ever moved the prop, never the animation
    -- so the left arm did the reaching while the nozzle hung at his side.
    Reported by Zona Comics.

  - Fixed: the siphon and pour animations could stick. They ended in six
    different places and each was trusted to take the right clip off him;
    reloading the script mid-siphon took none of them off at all, because that
    path does not go through a stage change. The poses now come off by stage,
    the way the pump sound already did.

  - Spluttering is a share of the tank rather than a fixed number of litres.
    At 0.6 L it was under one per cent of a saloon and nearly four of a
    motorbike -- the smaller the tank the less warning you got, which is
    backwards. Capped, so no vehicle splutters for more than about two litres.

  - The spilled fuel pool grows with how much is on the floor, rather than
    reaching its full size in four seconds and then stopping. Nine litres now
    looks like nine litres.

  - The can he stands on the ground while siphoning is the same model he
    carries -- the scuffed scenery can was a visibly different red next to the
    clean one on his back.

  - 214 settings in the ini, up from 207.
