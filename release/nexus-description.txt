<i>Persistent vehicle fuel for GTA V, with a refuel you walk through.</i>
by spitmux


<b>[ WHAT IT IS ]-------------------------------------------</b>

Every vehicle carries a real fuel tank. It empties as you drive, and it is still empty when you come back to that car tomorrow.

<b>Refuelling is not a menu.</b>
Walk to the pump -&gt; take the nozzle -&gt; walk it to the filler -&gt; fill -&gt; walk it back -&gt; hang it up.
The hose comes with you. Walk too far and it pulls the nozzle out of your hands. &gt;_&lt;

Runs on <b>Legacy and Enhanced from one build</b>.


<b>[ WHAT ELSE IS IN THERE ]--------------------------------</b>

<ul>
<li><b>[+] Siphon</b> fuel out of any car with a jerry can, or fill the can at a pump.</li>
<li><b>[+] Overflow.</b> A full can does not stop it. The spill pools at your feet, and it ignites. (o)</li>
<li><b>[+] Grades</b> -- Regular, Plus, Premium. The grade stays in the tank. A card at the pump shows what each costs there.</li>
<li><b>[+] Diesel</b> for trucks, buses, tractors and the fire engine. Costs more, goes further.</li>
<li><b>[+] Bikes</b> get a bike's tank -- about $17 to fill, same range as a car.</li>
<li><b>[+] Electric</b> vehicles are left alone entirely. No tank, no gauge, no fuel burned.</li>
<li><b>[+] Traffic</b> pulls into forecourts when it is low and already nearby.</li>
<li><b>[+] The gauge</b> sloshes with speed, chimes on reserve, and hides with the game's HUD.</li>
<li><b>[+] 25 stations</b> on the map, one legend entry between them.</li>
</ul>


<b>[ REQUIREMENTS ]-----------------------------------------</b>

<ul>
<li><b>[*] Script Hook V</b> for your edition.</li>
<li><b>[*] ScriptHookVDotNet 3</b> -- any build from <b>3.6.0</b> up. Nightlies and the Enhanced fork included.</li>
</ul>

On <b>GTA V Enhanced</b> it must be the Enhanced fork: <a href="https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/script-hook-v-net-enhanced">script-hook-v-net-enhanced</a>

<b>[!] There is no "3.9" to download.</b> The Enhanced fork reports 3.9.0.0 but is released as v1.1.x. To see what you have: right-click <i>ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll</i> -&gt; Properties -&gt; Details.

Nothing else is needed. <b>[!] Remove any other fuel mod first.</b>


<b>[ INSTALLING ]-------------------------------------------</b>

Copy the <i>scripts</i> folder from the zip into your GTA V folder:

<b>GTA V\</b>
<b>+-- scripts\</b>
<b>....+-- Fumes.dll</b>
<b>....+-- Fumes.ini</b>
<b>....+-- Fumes\</b>
<b>........+-- stations.json</b>
<b>........+-- icons\ ...... the HUD's pictures. Keep it.</b>

To uninstall, delete those. Nothing else is written and nothing in the game's files is touched.


<b>[ CONTROLS ]---------------------------------------------</b>

<ul>
<li><b>[SHIFT]+[F]</b> ... the settings menu, 217 of them. <b>[LB]+[D-pad Down]</b> on a controller.</li>
<li><b>[E]</b> ... take the nozzle, fill, hang up, pour the can.</li>
<li><b>[Q]</b> ... siphon, or fill the can at a pump.</li>
</ul>
