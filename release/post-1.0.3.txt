FUMES 1.0.3 -- short changelog for comments / the upload form
=============================================================

1.0.3 is up.

FIXED, the ones reported here:
  - An empty jerry can can be filled at a pump again. @AliG_15
  - The gauge can be moved with Bare Minimum installed. @VaporHaven
    @HunterBoys555
  - No more sparks and crawling along on an empty tank -- two causes
    stacked, ours and the game's own low-fuel routine. @GrandHaven
    @AliG_15
  - The Oppressor Mk II no longer hovers on an empty tank. @AliG_15
  - And the car no longer vanishes while you siphon from it.

NEW:
  - Fuel comes out of the nozzle. Hold FIRE with it in your hand away
    from a filler and petrol pours on the ground, charged at that
    pump's price, and the forecourt hazard treats it like any fill.
  - Every vehicle has its own thirst, worked out from its weight and
    engine rather than a table, so DLC and add-ons get a real figure.
    All of it adjustable: THIS VEHICLE on the FUEL page, and a new
    CLASSES page. @Asaseh @Aquaphobic
  - Reserve routes the GPS to the nearest station, in orange.
  - The low-fuel splutter is backfires and smoke and never touches
    the driving. He curses the car in his own voice when it is dry.
  - The hose is smooth -- 36 segments, not 14.

246 settings. Drop it over the old one, nothing needs a new save.
